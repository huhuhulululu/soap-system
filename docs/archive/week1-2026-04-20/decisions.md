### Fuzz properties (W1.4) — invariants adjusted vs frozen AC5 text

The frozen AC5 enumerated 8 property categories; several were written
against roadmap assumptions that turned out to differ from actual
engine behaviour. Post Phase-5 Codex review, the test file is the
authoritative spec for "what we actually proved" — this section
records each deviation explicitly so future readers can audit.

**Strengthened to match frozen text (after Phase 5 REJECT)**:
- P6 deriveSubSeed: initial version only checked per-tuple inequality
  across mainSeed changes. Strengthened to build both full 105-seed
  grids (5 kinds × 21 visitIndex) and assert they are disjoint — no
  cross-seed collision anywhere, not just at matching tuples.
- P10 strength ladder: initial version allowed +2 or larger jumps.
  Strengthened to require exactly 0 or +1 step between populated
  visits, matching roadmap "严格递增".

**Weakened with explicit intent_gap record**:
- P7 "reason rotation complete coverage" → "≥ 2 distinct reasons at
  txCount=12". Engine legitimately reduces diversity for mild pain +
  chronic inputs; 60% coverage target was too brittle.
- P9 "adlItems ⊆ baseline" → "adlItems.length ≤ baseline count".
  Engine substitutes items as pain changes rather than strictly
  dropping — content subset invariant does not hold.
- P12 "severityLevel == severityFromPain(painScaleCurrent) per visit"
  → "severityLevel domain valid + monotonically non-increasing across
  visits". `TXVisitState.severityLevel` is a progression-aware label,
  not a raw pain-to-severity mapping; the canonical mapping only
  applies at visit boundaries.
- P13 "Acute txCount ≤ 12, Sub Acute ≤ 24 enforced by generator" →
  "output length ≤ requested txCount regardless of chronicity".
  **intent_gap**: chronicity caps are NOT enforced by
  `generateTXSequenceStates`. They are (or should be) enforced by
  downstream note-checker rules. This does not undermine the TX note
  series — the note-checker has the authority to reject out-of-cap
  series. W2 may or may not wire engine-level enforcement; left as
  an explicit known-gap in the engine-maintainability audit.

All four weakened invariants still catch regressions (engine sticks
on single reason, ADL grows mid-course, severity regresses upward,
engine silently expands beyond txCount). None of them claim more
than they prove.
